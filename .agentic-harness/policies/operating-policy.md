> Linked owners: `AH-CONSTITUTION-001`, `AH-SPEC-COLLABORATION-001`, `AH-SPEC-WORK-001`, and `AH-SPEC-DELIVERY-001`

This file is navigation, not a competing Constitution. Exact released rules live in `.agentic-harness/core/` and are identified by `manifest.md`.

## Before Work

KISS applies to every phase. Read Collaboration§9 and Work§10 in the installed Core for the
smallest sufficient tooling, the two-failed-diagnostics approach decision and reuse of unaffected
evidence. A technical obstacle or broad correction permission does not authorize new infrastructure.
Use the existing Run/checkpoint for necessary decisions; do not add control artifacts for this rule.

For changed Agent behavior, route to Evaluation§5.1: one or two bounded work samples by default,
five minutes each plus one shared five-minute independent assessment; no automatic repeats.
Reuse cases/evidence, declare fresh subjects in the existing budget, and distinguish ordinary
tagged-source acceptance from explicitly selected extended formal assurance. Mechanical-only work
does not automatically need a model call. Missing evidence never becomes a pass.

From receipt of the Human task, including entry reading and preparation, apply
`.agentic-harness/core/work-management-and-execution.spec.md` §5.1. Read that task-wide duty section
now; do not wait for Run activation or ask the Human to repeat it. D0 routine under Collaboration
§4 uses existing task/diff/focused evidence with no Run/WP/Slice/Domain Binding/Spawn/progress form.
It may follow the authorized routine RC path through Staging under Delivery §2.2. Material D1–D3
retain10% updates/20% drift, including assigned non-author40/80 and risk/effort proportionality.
Work§5.1 applies that sequence once to the Human aggregate under the Lead. Delegated work reports
results/open work without duplicating percentages or waiting for its own40/80% approval.

Maintain the owner's compact coverage status in communication until a Run exists, then in the
existing Run progress section. Recheck due integrity, scope/authority, Agent/review, Human Gate,
capability, mutation-boundary, budget/stop and routed requirements before their action.
For new ordinary `1.3.1+` Runs, use the template's `task-controls` block; invoke `--check-run`
at start/resume and after relevant checked-input changes under Admission §4. Reuse unchanged
coverage/checks rather than rewriting eight entries or rerunning a command at each edit. Missing evidence blocks affected continuation; disclose missed checks and actual later
resolution, never claim retroactive timeliness. No extra status file or universal tool enforcement
is implied. Preparation and installation-only/staged adoption gain no implementation authority.

## While Working

Optional Harness logging is disabled unless expressly enabled for this task. Work §9.1 and
`workflows/harness-logging.md` own the concise report, persistent on/off state and later requested
analysis. It introduces no extra task hierarchy, Agent, check or mandatory completion commit.

- For material multi-step work, maintain the one existing plan/worklist under Work §5.1 with
  original requirements, observed evidence, open work and next step. Link it in existing handover;
  after resume read it before choosing the next open item. No extra D0 plan or duplicate tracker.
- Stay inside task/Run scope and actual action authority. Original outcome, observed sources,
  assumptions and permitted operations are distinct; Staging-only execution does not imply
  Staging-only implementation. Harness leases are removed; repository and Product rules own
  their Git and deployment procedures.
- Do not use an Agent summary as start clearance. Changed assignment/authority requires a fresh
  pass; in-scope corrections and clearance metadata alone do not. Exact content review remains
  revision-bound. Keep approval metadata outside its payload; no parallel manual digest list.
- Continue the task-wide Work §5.1 progress, drift and duty coverage begun at entry; activation,
  correction and resume do not reset it. Retain cumulative budgets and report real evidence.
- Do not create parallel implementations for reviewers to consolidate. Reviewers challenge the creator's exact revision.
- Consolidate all findings for one revision, perform one coherent correction pass, and restrict closure review to affected findings. Route adjacent improvements and pre-existing debt to follow-up.
- Keep durable product behavior in owning global/domain/contract specs.
- For Greenfield work, stop detailed Product conception, UX structure, contracts, architecture, and
  planning until the Human Domain Decision makes the Foundation effective. For Brownfield work,
  require effective coverage or an approved context-specific legacy exception only for the Context
  being changed; do not block unrelated legacy areas.
- Keep Work Packages modular, but integrate/deploy them through their coherent vertical Delivery Slice; do not deploy every small package by default.
- Use independent Product/User, Architecture/Engineering, and Approval/Simplicity perspectives according to decision class.
- Prefer a strong first pass, reusable approved components, simple domain boundaries, and evidence over repeated speculative iterations.
- Stop for missing authority, dangerous side effects, exhausted correction budget, integrity drift, or Human Gate.
- Stop before extra Agents or rounds unless an exact Human-approved conditional allocation or
  amendment already covers the need. Collaboration §4.1 permits one focused third-working-reviewer
  assignment for a critical competence gap or unresolved material dissent, not automatic fan-out.
- Before costly material workflow-UI creation, use Design §7's source-bound interaction meaning
  and early assigned-reviewer walk-through; reuse unchanged flows with a justified delta.
- For Domain work, reference sufficient source meaning and model only named gaps/conflicts.
  Human briefs foreground actual choices, new learning, consequences and recommendation, not IDs.
- Never create, reveal, share, or retain a Production Product Admin credential. Real privileged enrollment/assignment is an exact Human-authorized external action; Staging test/Admin/automation and cloud-operator identities remain separate.
- In a Mission, balance `Bias for Action`, `Dive Deep`, `High Standards`, and `Deliver Results`: act reversibly, investigate deeply without broadening mutation, meet concrete standards without gold-plating, and finish observable outcomes without weakening evidence or authority.
- Before a Mission Package's first mutation, bind the compact intent and remaining budget. At its checkpoint, retain the readable intent reference (pre1.5.1: digest), actual outcome/path mapping, evidence/invariants, assumptions, findings/follow-ups, dependency drift, next item, and one `continue | stop | escalate | complete` decision. Do not create per-edit ceremony.
- Correct ordinary mapped defects autonomously within the declared attempt/review budget. A safe reversible assumption records its validation trigger and fallback; materially different Product/business/security/cost choices escalate. A reviewer finding cannot add scope without expansion authority.
- A defect introduced by a correction remains autonomously correctable while the same user outcome, Scope Lock, risk boundary and finite Run/Mission authority remain. The Lead may `correct`, `simplify`, `rollback`, or `reimplement` from current evidence; record the expected user benefit and validation/rollback trigger in the existing checkpoint. Do not create a nested correction state.
- A non-Mission Run never draws Mission reserve. After exhaustion, only an exact Human-authorized
  Contract revision may enlarge cumulative ceilings; record the reason and authority in that same
  Contract. Git preserves the predecessor.
- Confirm critical findings from evidence/quorum; recurrence triggers convergence, no independent
  numeric chain ceiling. Work §10 owns one finite reserve: D0 one, ordinary1.5 three, Mission its
  expressly approved total; old tighter limits remain. Each coherent correction includes its
  tests/closure, including the last allowed cycle. Preparation/initial work has a finite work bound.
- Preserve consumption across resume and administrative reentry under Work §7. Do not create
  successor objects solely for exhausted local counters or status; no fresh authority from spare
  reserve after the original task actually ended.

## Completion

Check the original requirement sources and Human changes against the existing plan and observed
evidence before declaring completion. Missing required work stays open; only its owning Human
authority changes the promised scope. The independent reviewer checks each original criterion,
not just the diff or a creator summary.

Report only the highest evidenced completion level. Artifact §4 makes the final immutable material
Review the acceptance/spec-activation source, discoverable via Deployment history and RC. No
mandatory spec move, completion commit or redeploy for metadata. Routine uses its own result
evidence without material acceptance. An explicitly requested notification remains open if failed.
Use Cases → User Flows → screens/states drive UI; reuse unchanged flows for small copy changes.

 A Package may stop at `slice_ready`; deploy-relevant technical acceptance belongs to the exact Product Delivery Release Candidate for its Slice and needs real Staging behavior, critical-user review, mockup comparison where applicable, logs/resources/cost inspection, independent review, and technical acceptance. A final candidate only reserves its intended Product version; only a successful Human-approved Production promotion publishes that version and immutable Product tag.

New/continuing work requires the responsible Agent's actual current start clearance. A completed
historical omission may be disposed only through Collaboration §1.2 / Ordinary Admission §6.1's
genuine retrospective assessment and exact Human risk decision. That is no earlier pass and no
authority for new work. Deterministic validation proves recorded structure, not semantic truth.

Human approval is not required for each safe local step. It is required only by the Constitution, selected profile, or exact Run/action contract.

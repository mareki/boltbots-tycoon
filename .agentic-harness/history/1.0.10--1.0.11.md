## Change And Product Impact

Prospective ordinary non-Mission Runs pinned to `1.0.11` count every
`changes_requested -> active` transition against their existing correction-pass ceiling. A
correction-created defect remains ordinary in-scope work while finite authority remains, and the
Lead may choose the smallest evidenced correction, simplification, rollback or reimplementation
that preserves the same user problem and required outcome.

All four Mission-reserve fields remain present and exact `none` outside a complete Mission. When
existing cumulative authority is genuinely exhausted or a governed boundary must change, one
optional compact Human amendment may appear only on the exact successor edge where it increases
the existing lineage ceiling. The amendment is parent/digest/lineage/purpose/scope-bound and
cannot add its allocation twice.

## Recalibration

Compatible upgrade replaces only `.agentic-harness/**`; Product-owned Runs and history are not
rewritten. Runs completed under an earlier pinned Harness retain their historical meaning. Before
a new correction transition under `1.0.11`, retain the existing Run Contract, admission, Scope
Lock and cumulative lineage, and use a Human amendment only when the existing authority is
insufficient.

No Product, provider or deployment action is authorized. Expected unchanged provider visibility
continues through the existing bounded read-only verification/recheck path. Package 3 remains
outside this release.

## Change And Compatibility

New1.4.0 assignments use two working Agents: Creator and one independent Reviewer covering the
required perspectives, plus a separate temporary start-only Spawn Reviewer. A third working
reviewer requires a concrete critical competence gap or unresolved material dissent after one
clarification, exact prior finite allocation and genuine start review. A permissible tradeoff may
close at2/3 with an independent voice; an evidenced blocker or Human Gate cannot be voted away.
One review, one consolidated correction and one targeted closure are the default ceiling.

Material workflow UI resolves source-defined identity, grouping, status, action prerequisites,
decision scope and revision invalidation before expensive rendering. Simple unchanged flows need
only a short justified delta. Domain preparation reuses rich inputs, models concrete gaps and
keeps Human choices understandable. Explanation-only authority never initiates modeling.

KISS applies from entry through diagnosis and review. Reuse valid unaffected evidence; after two
unsuccessful ancillary diagnoses, decide the simplest bounded approach before continuing. A tool
failure or broad correction permission does not authorize infrastructure expansion. Existing
entry and role instructions carry this rule; no additional framework or control artifact is added.
These operating rules do not waive mandatory behavioral evidence or guarantee Agent compliance.

New1.4.0 ordinary work/tagged-source releases select one/two bounded work samples under Evaluation
§5.1, with finite calls/time and no automatic retries. Extended formal assurance is explicitly
selected for a concrete risk/claim; its old Case contracts and attestation machinery are unchanged.
This prospective Human-approved policy does not turn historical not_run Cases into passed evidence.

This is a Minor capability/default evolution, not a new Product schema or automatic Product
migration. Core-only replacement preserves Product-owned files and completed historical Runs.
Historical review/quorum rules retain their pins. The existing Domain-review block may bind a
Harness version for new review evidence; missing/older bindings retain the historical two-reviewer
requirement. Current templates supply the new rules without rewriting old contracts or evidence.

against installed1.4.0. For an active older Run, use the governed current re-entry/revision path;

an active UI interaction model or Domain decision really has a semantic gap before adding work.

bypass or automatic initialization/adoption is required.

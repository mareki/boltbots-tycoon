## Change And Product Impact

Harness `1.2.1` called supplied mockups binding but also allowed Agents to copy or reimplement only
selected design learning. That contradiction permitted semantic reconstruction of covered UI.

Harness `1.2.2` makes the precedence explicit:

- supplied mockups bind their covered observable contract unless a named aspect is explicitly
  classified illustrative;
- established Product-wide shell, Design System, global tokens, shared components and cross-page
  behavior remain binding for an existing Product unless an exact global-change outcome applies;
- general improvement authority cannot reinterpret a binding mockup; and
- technical runtime reimplementation preserves the observable contract.

## Recalibration From 1.2.1

- Replace only `.agentic-harness/**` through the authenticated upgrade path.
- Do not rewrite Product code, root instructions, Contracts, plans or historical evidence.
- For future mockup-backed work, treat covered mockup surfaces and established Product-wide UI
  owners as simultaneous binding inputs under the precedence above.
- Existing completed Runs remain valid under their pinned Harness version.

The Human Product Authority waived Spawn Review and independent Agent review for this bounded
release and accepted the reduced independent-challenge risk. No Product implementation,
deployment, provider, Production or customer-data action is authorized.

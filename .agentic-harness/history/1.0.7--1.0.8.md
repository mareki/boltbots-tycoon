## Change And Product Impact

New durable top-level Review records use
`docs/reviews/YYYYMMDD-<harness-semver>-<stable-logical-name>-review.md`. Raw Run-bound
artifacts below `docs/reviews/evidence/**` keep their existing immutable identities. The
released Review template carries the convention; Master validation rejects nonconforming
top-level names.

Compatible upgrade replaces only `.agentic-harness/**`. It does not rename or edit any
Product-owned historical Review or Evidence file.

## Recalibration

Use the new filename for Review records created after upgrade. Existing Product records need
no migration and remain valid. No Product behavior, plan, Run, deployment, or release policy
changes automatically.

## Change And Product Impact

New state-mutating ordinary non-Mission Runs use one compact run-kind-specific Admission
Projection in the existing immutable Run Contract. The installed validator requires exact
Harness-version, target, Scope Lock, capability/integrity, authority, reasoning, budget,
environment and lease bindings before an event may validly enter `active`.

The active event embeds the exact generated Admission Report and digest. Later events bind that
report plus their transition-time repository observation. Contract admission, event-chain
integrity and semantic review remain separate claims; deterministic admission never claims
semantic success.

## Recalibration

Compatible upgrade replaces only `.agentic-harness/**`; Product-owned Runs and history are not
rewritten. Runs completed under an earlier pinned Harness retain their historical meaning. Before
new work or valid continuation under `1.0.10`, instantiate the released ordinary Admission
Projection, retain the cumulative `1.0.9` budget lineage where the outcome continues, acquire the
applicable lease and generate a fresh report through the installed validator.

This upgrade adds no cloud/provider fields, default reviewer, deployment authority, Product
mutation or Package-2/Package-3 behavior.

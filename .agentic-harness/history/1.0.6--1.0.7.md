## Change And Product Impact

Products now own an explicit Product Release Policy independently from the installed Harness version. SemVer is the default; a Product may approve another completely defined deterministic monotonic scheme when its delivery model makes that more truthful. Final Product Delivery Release Candidates reserve an intended Product version/tag under the exact Policy revision. Verification Candidates carry no Product version. Only successful Human-approved Production promotion publishes the immutable version/tag and binds it to the Product Release Record, Deployment Ledger, exact candidate, artifacts, and current-serving state.

The compatible upgrade still replaces only `.agentic-harness/**`. It does not create, infer, approve, or edit a Product-owned Release Policy, version source, application metadata, release tag, plan, RC, or evidence record.

## Recalibration

Assess only release-capable current or future Product work:

- record `no_change` when an existing approved Product-owned policy already defines the release unit, deterministic scheme/grammar, compatibility meaning, source of truth, immutable tag, candidate/publication, component, migration/rollback, and evidence rules required by `AH-SPEC-PRODUCT-RELEASE-001`;
- record `required_before_continue` before the next final Product Delivery Release Candidate or Production promotion when no compliant approved Policy exists, or when an active final candidate lacks the required `reserved_unpublished` version/tag/Policy binding;
- use `deferred` only when no release-capable work can occur before the named trigger;
- use `human_decision` when choosing the scheme or compatibility audience materially changes Product promises.

Do not rewrite Product files automatically. Existing published Product tags/releases remain immutable. Work completed under an older pinned Harness keeps its historical evidence; only a separately authorized Product change may create or update the Product Release Policy and affected future candidate/release artifacts.

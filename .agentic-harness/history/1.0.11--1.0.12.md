## Change And Product Impact

This patch corrects Master-only provenance validation for immutable Runs that produced a Harness
release. A producer remains admissible after publication only when its introduction commit
already listed it, its bytes remain unchanged, and its pinned version is the immediate predecessor
of that release. Human-approved `high` governance overrides are bound to the exact projected
decision authority.

Installed Product validation and Product-owned files do not gain a producer exception. Ordinary
admission, correction authority and finite budgets are unchanged.

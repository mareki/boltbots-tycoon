## Change And Product Impact

Active Runs report outcome-based progress at every 10 percentage points and perform drift
checks at 20/40/60/80/100. The 40/80 checks receive one assigned non-author validation.
Critical findings require the class quorum, retain one causal-chain identity, and trigger a
drift/convergence decision at the third same-chain finding instead of an automatic stop.

New Runs use one cumulative budget lineage for Agent activations, review cycles, and remediation
attempts. A successor, replacement, correction, renamed Run, or changed team carries its
predecessor consumption forward; validator rule `AHV-BUDGET-006` fails closed on reset, weakened
constants, invalid arithmetic, or broken predecessor continuity.

## Recalibration

Compatible upgrade replaces only `.agentic-harness/**`; Product files and historical Runs remain
unchanged. Before authorizing new work under 1.0.9, instantiate the released Execution Run
template and its convergence-budget projection. Existing Runs retain the behavior of their pinned
Harness release; continuing the same outcome in a 1.0.9 Run must preserve the known cumulative
budget rather than treating the upgrade as a reset.

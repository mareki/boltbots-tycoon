## Change And Product Impact

Product validation now scans Git-tracked plus untracked nonignored Product files instead of every
file present in the worktree. Ignored dependency, cache, generated and build artifacts therefore do
not create Product link, nested-Agent-instruction or secret findings. Authenticated
`.agentic-harness/**` integrity remains a raw-filesystem check.

Older terminal Contracts are not revalidated against later artifact schemas only when their lower
Harness pin, Contract bytes and complete digest-bound terminal event ledger are proven in Git under
that pinned release. Current, non-terminal, changed or unproven artifacts remain fail-closed.

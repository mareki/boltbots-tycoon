## 1. Ordinary Correction

Work §10 is the single owner of correction counting and limits for Harness1.5. Ordinary D1–D3
uses a default total of three cycles; D0 one without a Run; a larger Mission uses its explicitly
approved finite total. Existing tighter authority is retained. The existing task/Run section holds
limit, consumed/remaining amount and the finite work bound including preparation/initial work.

Inside that authority the Lead may correct, simplify, roll back or reimplement without another
Human decision while outcome, allowed paths, non-goals, external actions and material risk remain
unchanged. Bundle related findings into one cause correction and affected verification. A new
failure after that verification consumes the next cycle, including correction-created defects.
Renaming/resume/upgrade cannot reset consumption or reclassify a failed candidate as first-pass.

## 2. Stop Boundary

Reserve exhaustion prevents another mutation, not tests or targeted closure for the final allowed
cycle. New scope, missing capability/authority or unsafe effects stop at their affected boundary.
A finite prebounded safe unchanged transient retry or read-only observation is not a technical
correction. The original failure stays visible. A new configuration/IAM/artifact change is one.
The two-failed-diagnostics approach decision remains; no independent numeric causal-chain ceiling.

## 3. Human-Approved Increase And Reentry

An increase needs exact prior Human authority recorded with the finite value, reason and consumed
amount in an incremented existing Contract, then current admission. Work §7 permits1.5 administrative
reentry only for the still-unfulfilled original task within valid mandate, working bound and
reserve, with non-author approval of its material revision. A finished task or spare reserve grants
no perpetual repair authority. Published bytes remain immutable and Production approval stays
candidate-specific. No amendment projection, budget ledger or replacement Work trio is needed.

## 4. Missions

Every Mission item references the existing authoritative Mission total and remaining consumption.
There is no automatic three cycles per Package/Run and no additional local correction ceiling.
An ordinary task cannot borrow another Mission's authority. New1.5 rules never enlarge historical
consumed authorizations; current activation carries their real remaining limits.

## 5. Verification

Tests prove that:

- in-scope corrections continue only while the finite ceiling remains;
- the next correction fails after exhaustion;
- a new Run ID does not reset the same outcome's consumption;
- a Human-approved Contract revision can add one finite amount; and
- no report, event, amendment projection or document hash is required.

Deterministic validation proves recorded limits and references only. Product value and whether a
change stays inside the same outcome remain review judgments.
